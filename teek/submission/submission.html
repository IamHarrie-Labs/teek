Teek

A real-time market where speed is worthless: sealed MagicBlock ER batches clear at one price, so the sniper bot visibly loses.

What it does

Teek makes market microstructure visible. The top demo compares a normal continuous order book against a sealed uniform-price batch auction. In the continuous book, a faster sniper bot captures stale quotes. In Teek, orders are routed through MagicBlock hosted Ephemeral Rollups, sealed into a batch, cleared with VRF, and committed back to Solana devnet.

Why MagicBlock

  • Ephemeral Rollups make repeated real-time order writes practical.
  • Delegation moves real market state into the hosted ER.
  • MagicBlock VRF drives asynchronous clearing callbacks.
  • Commit + undelegate settles the final state back to Solana.

How to demo

  1. Open the Teek UI.
  2. Watch the sniper profit on the continuous-book side.
  3. Click Run Live Demo.
  4. Wait for the progress log to delegate, trade, clear, and commit.
  5. Show the reveal line: cleared 10 units at price 100 (2 fills).
