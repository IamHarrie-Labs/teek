# Demo video script

Aim for about 2 minutes 45 seconds. The lines in quotes are what you say. Everything else is what's on screen.

A launch takes a few minutes of real waiting between steps, so record a fresh launch ahead of time and cut out the waiting. The two finished launches on the site cover the parts you can't wait for.

## Before you record

Open https://teek-navy.vercel.app/launch.html, press Use demo wallet and then Get test funds, so the wallet has SOL. In the Studio, publish a launch with short times, like deposits closing in 3 minutes and bidding closing 3 minutes after that. Keep the evidence page open in another tab.

## The problem (about 20 seconds)

Show the home page.

"When a new token launches on Solana, bots buy in the very first second. Everyone after them pays more, often buying from those same bots. Speed is the whole game, and people lose it. Teek takes the race away."

## How it works (about 25 seconds)

Scroll through the "how it works" part of the home page.

"The creator publishes rules that can't change. Buyers put money into an escrow and bid privately inside MagicBlock's Private Ephemeral Rollup. When bidding closes, one transaction creates the Meteora pool and buys with everyone's money together. So everyone pays the same price, and if the minimum isn't reached, everyone gets refunded."

## Making a launch (about 30 seconds)

Show the Launch Studio with your short times. Point at the example outcomes and the cost note on the review step.

"This is the Launch Studio. I pick the raise limits and the timing, and before publishing it uses Meteora's own maths to show what the opening would look like at different demand levels. Publishing locks these rules on Solana."

## Joining and bidding privately (about 30 seconds)

Show depositing, then saving a bid once bidding opens, then Check my bid.

"I deposit into the launch's escrow. That part is public. Once bidding opens I choose how much to actually spend. That bid lives inside MagicBlock's private rollup, and only I can read it. We tested this with a second wallet and with the creator, and neither could see it."

## The opening (about 30 seconds)

Show Close bidding, then Request randomness, then Complete opening purchase, then the record card. Open the opening transaction on the explorer.

"Bidding closes and the bids come back to Solana. MagicBlock VRF gives a random number for sharing out the leftovers fairly. Then this one transaction creates the Meteora pool and makes the opening buy. You can see both inside it. Nobody could trade before this, because the pool didn't exist."

## Claiming and the refund case (about 20 seconds)

Show Claim tokens and refund, then switch to the refunded launch on the evidence page.

"I claim my tokens and the part of my deposit that wasn't used. And here's a launch that missed its minimum. No pool was made, and every deposit went back. The site checks the actual payment history to prove it."

## Honest limits and close (about 10 seconds)

"Deposits are public and bids are only hidden until bidding closes. It's on devnet and hasn't been audited yet. Teek gives private bids and one fair opening, and you can try it yourself at teek-navy.vercel.app."
