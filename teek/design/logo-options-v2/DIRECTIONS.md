# Teek logo exploration — second collection

Five concept previews generated with the built-in image-generation tool.
These are concept images, not production vector masters. No website logo was replaced.

Shared prompt: Design a premium private token-launch platform identity for Teek.
One centered symbol and wordmark per image, ample space, custom typography,
flat vector-like shapes and precise edges. Avoid stacked squares, keys,
padlocks, coins, shields, rockets, mockups and decorative copy.

1. **Aperture**: broken circular mark, hidden lowercase t in negative space,
   ivory on charcoal with coral accent and a lowercase wordmark.
2. **Ribbon T**: continuous folded ribbon forming an uppercase T,
   oxblood on ivory with architecturally cut custom letters.
3. **Twin Gate**: opposing curved forms defining an abstract doorway,
   ink on warm paper and linked E letter crossbars.
4. **Orbit**: two off-axis orbital arcs with a small coral dot,
   ivory on charcoal and rounded lowercase typography.
5. **Cut Wordmark**: typography-only TEEK, diagonal cuts through both E
   letters and a restrained coral stroke on ivory.
